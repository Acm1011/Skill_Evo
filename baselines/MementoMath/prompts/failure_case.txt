You are building a reusable Memento-style memory library for math reasoning.

You will be given:
- a math problem
- its topic
- whether the student's answer is correct
- the ground truth answer
- the student's solution

Your task:
1. Extract a short reusable recovery plan from this failed trajectory.
2. Explain how a solver should avoid the mistake and re-check the solution.
3. Keep the plan general enough to help similar future problems.

Return JSON only with this schema:
{
  "case_summary": "short summary of the failure pattern",
  "plan": [
    {"id": 1, "description": "step"},
    {"id": 2, "description": "step"}
  ],
  "takeaway": "one short lesson about what to avoid or verify",
  "tags": ["tag1", "tag2"]
}

Rules:
- 2 to 5 plan steps.
- Emphasize verification and failure avoidance.
- Do not include markdown fences or extra explanation.
